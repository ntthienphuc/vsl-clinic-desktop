# Natural conversation and pilot robustness

Research and source audit: **2 November 2026**. This is a proposed design, not an implemented feature or a new release. The user reports that current realtime performance is good; preserve that fast path. No new microphone, webcam, new-signer or clinical validation was performed for this note.

## Decision

Prioritize evidence-aware correction, sign/non-sign discrimination, stable incremental output and short clarification. Keep capture, recognition and avatar playback local. Use language reasoning selectively after perception, with bounded work and cancellation; do not put an LLM in every frame's critical path.

Naturalness includes waiting through a hesitation, ignoring irrelevant activity, allowing interruption and correcting a misunderstanding. Fluent output alone is insufficient. Separate what was observed, what is tentatively recognized, and what has been committed to another person.

## Fresh audit of 0.4.0 source

| Area | Observed implementation | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speech endpoint | Silero, 32 ms frames, 576 ms silence endpoint, 12 s ceiling in `backend/speech.py` | Fast acoustic segmentation; a pause inside a sentence can still split meaning. |
| ASR | PhoWhisper base INT8, two CPU threads, beam size 3, one returned transcript, segment-level log probability | The application does not retain three alternative transcripts or calibrated word confidence. |
| Language assistance | Qwen3 0.6B text-only selection among retrieved phrase IDs, on demand and requiring confirmation | It does not listen again to audio or recover candidates absent from retrieval. |
| Patient segmentation | Hand-motion votes, lowered hands or 700 ms hold, then 1500 ms conversation-turn pause | A motion threshold is not a trained sign/non-sign detector; fluent coarticulation can defeat word boundaries. |
| Recognition | Isolated 400-class SL-GCN, top three returned scores, hand-coverage adjustments | Closed vocabulary; adjusted scores are not calibrated probabilities of correctness. |
| Patient delivery | Top prediction is appended immediately; low score/poor sampling marks uncertainty | The offline voice player currently speaks an uncertainty cue and then the predicted word. |
| Patient sentence | Conservative rule grouping after the turn; contextual alternatives remain separate | No unrestricted sign-to-sentence understanding is present. |
| Avatar | 67 signs, 65 canonical phrase entries; question act/nonmanual metadata and brow animation exist | This is not unrestricted VSL grammar or validated comprehension of all composed sentences. |

## Doctor to patient: proposed pipeline

1. **Keep acoustic capture responsive.** Show an interim transcript. Add a bounded semantic endpoint layer after acoustic pauses: wait through an incomplete idea or self-correction, but release a complete supported clause promptly. Do not treat punctuation generated by ASR as proof that a turn ended.
2. **Preserve recognition evidence.** Retain a small n-best list, timestamps and acoustic scores. CTranslate2 supports `num_hypotheses`, scores and reuse of encoded features; an adapter can expose alternatives without three independent full ASR passes. Actual overhead and candidate diversity still need measurement. [CTranslate2 Whisper API](https://opennmt.net/CTranslate2/python/ctranslate2.models.Whisper.html)
3. **Retrieve from both words and phrases.** Add reviewed phonetic confusions and context-specific synonyms. `cạm ơn → cảm ơn` is a plausible repair candidate, not an unconditional string replacement. Vietnamese tones and similar spelling can change meaning; never remove all accents to decide equivalence.
4. **Use a constrained language model when needed.** Let it rank acoustically plausible candidates, identify uncertain spans, or suggest a short clarification. Keep raw ASR alongside proposed corrections. Text-only Qwen cannot supply missing acoustic evidence; a larger text model alone does not fix this.
5. **Check meaning before signing.** Preserve speech act, subject, action, body location, negation, quantity, unit and time. Every meaningful slot must be represented by a reviewed sign plan or explicitly identified as unsupported. Do not extract only words found in the bank. Lexical coverage is not semantic coverage.
6. **Commit deliberately.** Low-risk validated repairs may become automatic after held-out evaluation. Unclear numbers, left/right, negation, drugs or missing clinical concepts require a local clarification. Do not silently send text instead of signs. A doctor can explicitly choose the existing text-only route.

Example target behavior: `Y tá ơi, đo nhiệt độ giúp tôi nhé` can remove conversational particles while retaining the measurement request and recipient. `Đừng đo nhiệt độ` must not become the same sign sequence. `Tay trái… à, tay phải` must remain provisional until the self-correction settles. If an earlier clause has already been delivered, show an explicit correction; do not silently rewrite history.

Automatic repair is conditional on evidence, not a guarantee that every misspelling will be repaired. An n-best list can still omit the correct answer.

## Patient to doctor: proposed pipeline

1. **Keep the current inexpensive motion gate** as an activity proposal mechanism.
2. **Add a learned activity/rejection stage** trained on real sign attempts, incidental movements and transitions. Distinguish non-sign activity, a potentially valid sign outside the vocabulary, ambiguous in-vocabulary signs and missing tracking. A regional or personal variant is not automatically an incorrectly performed sign.
3. **Retain raw logits and candidate history.** Calibrate on unseen signers/sessions. Test temporal agreement and boundary stability alongside visual quality. A softmax threshold alone is not reliable rejection of unfamiliar movements. The current hand-coverage-scaled top-three scores must not be treated as a normalized full posterior.
4. **Publish incremental words.** A clear completed sign is committed and spoken immediately, without waiting for the sentence. An ambiguous candidate appears in the same live line with a visible tentative state; it is not spoken as fact. A short refinement window may resolve it. If not, ask for that sign again or show two evidence-supported alternatives.
5. **Use context only to rank visual alternatives.** A doctor's question may help distinguish two visually plausible candidates. It must not create a symptom absent from those candidates or force an expected answer. Prevent feedback loops from the system's own earlier guesses.
6. **Finalize the sentence separately.** Preserve raw glosses and show any fluent Vietnamese paraphrase as a separate rendering. Keep uncertainty attached to the relevant words. Do not invent a subject, symptom, negation, time or quantity to make the sentence sound complete.

A word's lifecycle should be `candidate → provisional → committed`, with explicit `rejected`, `needs_clarification` and correction events. Stable words retain IDs. Revision of a tentative suffix does not replay all earlier audio. End/reset and interruptions cancel obsolete work using the existing session/epoch guards.

Important edge cases: repeated signs can be meaningful, so deduplication cannot erase every adjacent repetition; static signs must not require large motion; one-handed signs must not require two visible hands; temporary occlusion must not manufacture a boundary. The current recognizer selects 27 pose/hand joints, not a full facial-expression representation. Facial grammar cannot be recovered reliably by a text-only LLM after those observations have been discarded.

## Interaction and avatar

Use one compact live line and the existing chat. During uncertainty, show a specific message such as `Chưa rõ từ cuối, bạn làm lại giúp nhé` rather than adding many pending cards. Patient-side recovery should use a simple visual cue, familiar replay/reference where available, or an explicit text fallback.

Documented realtime APIs illustrate useful interaction controls: OpenAI Semantic VAD varies turn completion with utterance content; Gemini Live exposes interruption and, on supported models, selective response to relevant audio. These are public API capabilities, not proof of consumer ChatGPT/Gemini internals or verified Vietnamese sign-language ability. [OpenAI VAD](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/realtime-vad), [Gemini Live capabilities](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/live-api/capabilities)

Transfer the principles: bounded waiting, revisable interim text, clean cancellation and an explicit point of commitment. A normal avatar interruption should preserve the current sign's meaningful stroke where possible; an explicit Stop must act immediately. Do not interrupt the avatar for every detected movement. Keep phrase IDs and playback acknowledgments so the doctor can distinguish queued, playing, completed and interrupted content.

Current patient-word audio deliberately pauses the doctor microphone to prevent self-transcription. This avoids feedback but is not full duplex. A later full-duplex mode needs acoustic echo cancellation with the app's playback reference, plus real-speaker tests; simply leaving the mic open can reintroduce duplicate/ghost speech. A headset/directional microphone is a useful pilot condition, not a software validation substitute.

For avatar naturalness, protect handshape, contact, stroke timing and meaningful holds; improve transition motion around them. Question facial animation already exists, but grammatical comprehensibility still needs fluent-signer review. Sign-D2C researches learned transition synthesis with a conditional diffusion model; it is a longer-term reference, not a model already installed here. [Sign-D2C, CVPR 2025 / author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16810)

## Research directly relevant to this project

| Primary source | What informs the design | Transfer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Towards Online Continuous Sign Language Recognition and Translation, EMNLP 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.619/) | Sign dictionary, isolated recognizer trained for online sliding windows, explicit background handling and deduplication. | Its dictionary is derived using continuous-data alignment; not equivalent to feeding our existing isolated model arbitrary windows. VSL training/evaluation is required. |
| [ReSCUE, arXiv October 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03022) | Training for partial inputs/non-sign pauses, stabilized re-translation and sentence commitment. | Authors identify early/late commitment and silence-detection failures. Runtime is measured on RTX 3090, not this laptop; use the ideas before considering the full model. |
| [Scaling Up Sign Spotting Through Sign Language Dictionaries, IJCV 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-022-01589-6) | Learn to locate dictionary signs inside coarticulated video using multiple supervision sources. | Dictionary recordings differ from natural continuous signing; a large QIPEDC keypoint bank alone does not solve that gap. |
| [Pose-Based Sign Language Spotting via an End-to-End Encoder Architecture, WSLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.wslp-main.10/) | Lightweight pose-based query-sign presence prediction. | Reports 61.88% accuracy / 60.00% F1 on its task; useful exploratory reference, not sufficient evidence for dependable clinic translation. |
| [Error Correction by Paying Attention to Both Acoustic and Confidence References, Interspeech 2024](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/shu24_interspeech.html) | Use acoustic evidence and confidence across n-best hypotheses to locate/correct errors. | Requires more than free text rewriting; gains on that paper's data are not established Vietnamese gains. |
| [Listen Again and Choose the Right Answer, Findings ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.37/) | Multimodal correction uses source speech and constrained cloze-style reasoning. | Its SpeechGPT-based approach is not our small text-only Qwen model. |

These papers motivate experiments. None establishes that our app reliably translates spontaneous VSL in a clinic, nor that a general live multimodal model can replace the current recognizer without evaluation.

## Data and validation before enabling automatic behavior

Build a small, versioned pilot challenge set with fluent signers, keeping signers and recording sessions separate between fitting, threshold selection and final testing. Include natural variants and disability-related movement differences. Capture consented, purpose-limited examples; do not silently retain clinical conversation recordings.

| Group | Required examples | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASR noise/repair | `cảm ơn`, regional pronunciation, fan noise, distant speech, incidental nearby conversation | Correction precision, harmful edits, unresolved cases and semantic-slot preservation; not WER alone. |
| Meaning contrast | Questions vs negation; left/right; one/two; time and units; self-corrections | Wrong automatic commitments and clarification rate, reported separately by risk group. |
| Non-sign activity | Adjust glasses, scratch face, drink water, use phone, turn body, point, rest hands, other people moving | False word emissions per non-sign minute and missed genuine sign attempts. |
| Natural signing | Slow/static signs, fast coarticulation, deliberate repetition, one-handed signs, partial occlusion, unseen variants/OOV signs | Word errors, boundary errors, rejection quality and repeat-request burden on held-out users. |
| Conversation | Mid-clause pauses, interruption, long turns, patient-word audio with open mic, late model results, End/reset | Premature cuts, duplicated speech/playback, stale delivery and correct cancellation. |
| Real system load | Existing known-good workload, then ASR + camera + avatar + selective language assist | Median and p95 end-to-end latency, sampling quality, queue growth, output revisions and RAM/CPU use. |

Report time to first visible candidate, time to stable word, time to voice onset and sentence finalization separately. Fast provisional output is not the same as fast correct output. Pair automatic-acceptance error with coverage/clarification burden; rejecting everything is not success. Use confidence intervals and disclose sample size. A small no-error rehearsal is not proof of zero pilot risk.

## Implementation order

1. **Instrument without changing decisions:** bounded hypothesis evidence, lifecycle events and replayable cases; record no sensitive raw audio/video by default. Establish an unchanged-app baseline under the user's current good realtime conditions.
2. **Doctor-side recovery:** word-and-phrase retrieval, small n-best exposure, reviewed phonetic repair candidates, meaning checks and bounded adaptive endpoints. Compare against existing behavior before enabling automatic low-risk edits.
3. **Patient commitment and rejection:** keep immediate clear-word display/readout; delay only uncertain audio. Train a small temporal sign-activity model with hard negatives and calibrate on held-out signers. Evaluate unknown signs separately from non-sign gestures.
4. **Continuous VSL:** collect natural multi-sign utterances and transitions, then train/evaluate an online recognizer using the EMNLP architecture as a reference. The 400-class boundary remains until a trained and validated model changes it.
5. **Comprehension and full duplex:** fluent-signer review of composed clauses/nonmanuals, followed by echo-cancellation and interruption tests. Learned avatar transition synthesis is an independent later experiment.

Expand the QIPEDC bank according to actual unsupported intents observed during testing, preserving regional variants and provenance. More avatar entries expand doctor-side expression only; they do not automatically add patient recognition classes. No new EXE, model replacement, Git commit, push or deployment accompanies this research note.
